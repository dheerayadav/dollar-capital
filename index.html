    Upcoming expiries

    How these are built: credit spreads use the nearest expiry with at least 2 days left, sell about 0.8 of the expected move away and buy about 0.4 of the expected move further out (at least 2 strikes). Debit spreads use the first expiry with at least a week left, buy the at-the-money strike and sell about 0.6 of the expected move away. Expiries that fall on a holiday move to the previous trading day. Premiums, breakevens and chances are estimates from a standard option-pricing formula with VIX as the volatility (Bank Nifty is scaled up by its own recent swings). Real prices differ, so check the live option chain on Kite. Amounts are in index points; multiply by the lot size. A study template, not advice.

    How this page works

    What breadth is

    An index like the Nifty 50 is driven by its largest companies. Breadth counts what all the stocks did: how many rose, how many made new one-year highs, how many are above their moving averages. This page uses the Nifty 200 stocks, counted equally. The list is today's Nifty 200 members, so older history leaves out companies that have since dropped out of the index.

    How to read it

    Each tile has a meter: the marker shows where today's reading sits against every trading day since . Far left is weaker than almost every day, far right is stronger. The small table shows the same reading a week, a month and three months ago. Watch the direction over weeks rather than single days.

    Words used on this page
    • DMA: daily moving average, the average close over the last 5 or 50 sessions.
    • 52-week high/low: the highest or lowest price over the last 252 sessions.
    • Expected vs actual: India VIX (what options imply for the year ahead) against the Nifty's actual swings over the last 20 sessions, annualised.
    • Monthly breakout: a close above the highest high (or below the lowest low) of the previous calendar month, or of the previous 3 months.
    • VIX lean: compares today's VIX with every past reading: how high it is, how far it sits above the Nifty's actual swings, whether it is rising fast, and how often VIX at similar levels turned out higher than the swings that followed.
    • Washout: fewer than 30% of stocks above their 50 DMA.
